# Witnet: The Oracle Making Physical Infrastructure Trustless and Verifiable

[<mark style="background-color:purple;">**DePIN**</mark>](#user-content-fn-1)[^1] <mark style="background-color:purple;">projects live or die by the reliability and verifiability of real-world data.</mark> Whether you’re coordinating sensors, devices, hardware operators, bandwidth providers, geolocation proofs, or energy contributors, you need an oracle that can’t be gamed, censored, or controlled by a central actor. **That’s where Witnet stands apart.**

With **Witnet**, every data request is defined _on-chain_—including source lists, filtering, and aggregation methods—so device operators and network contributors can’t manipulate inputs or exploit off-chain blind spots. Your infrastructure logic remains fully transparent and enforcement-ready.

Witnet also lets you **deploy custom data feeds permissionlessly**, without waiting for an oracle vendor to approve your use case. This means DePIN networks can move at hardware speed: plug in new device types, integrate new sensors, or add new geography-specific feeds instantly.

Fees are paid using the chain’s **native gas token**, eliminating the need for external ERC-20 tokens or proprietary oracle coins. This simplifies UX for device operators, reduces friction for onboarding new hardware nodes, and keeps costs aligned with your actual network load—not a corporate pricing model.

Every oracle query is permanently recorded on the Witnet blockchain, giving your network **end-to-end traceability**. You get a provable audit trail that shows exactly which data came from where and when—ideal for preventing fraud, double-counting, spoofing, or sensor tampering.

Witnet’s **trust model** is also built for resilient physical networks. Randomized commit-reveal committees resolve each query, offering natural protection against collusion. Manipulating a result requires controlling more than two-thirds of all staked supply; censoring data requires more than one-third. For DePINs that rely on thousands of distributed devices, this level of economic protection is critical.

<mark style="background-color:yellow;">In an ecosystem where infrastructure operators must trust each other—yet often can’t—</mark><mark style="background-color:yellow;">**Witnet provides a cryptographically verifiable bridge between the physical world and your on-chain logic**</mark><mark style="background-color:yellow;">. No permissions. No bottlenecks. No opaque operators.</mark>

Instead of a central company, individuals are incentivized with cryptocurrency tokens to provide resources like storage, bandwidth, or energy, creating a decentralized and community-owned model for services such as wireless networks, data storage, and more.</mark>&#x20;
